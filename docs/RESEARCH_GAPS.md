# Research and External Gaps

## Legal and regulatory content

- California form/title/effective-date sources and controlled update process.
- Business-day/deadline rules, service extensions, venue-specific practices, and attorney-approved calculation examples.
- Recording, transcription, SMS consent, retention, and client-specific restrictions.
- Retention, legal hold, breach notice, privilege, and client-delivery requirements by firm/client/data class.

## Provider and contract gaps

- Microsoft Graph scopes, mailbox model, subscription/delta behavior, throttling, revocation, and delivery evidence.
- MerusCase relationship, OAuth/endpoints, export samples, limits, write-back behavior, and source reconciliation.
- EAMS/JET or approved third-party transmission authority, test environment, forms, receipt/rejection contract.
- Malware/OCR/AI provider security review, region, retention, training use, BAA/DPA, subprocessors, and cost.
- Noted, Verbatim, telephony/SMS workflow/API/consent/billing contracts.

## Product validation gaps

- Design-partner interviews by role, daily workflow observation, billing guideline samples, and migration inventories.
- Accessibility testing with keyboard and assistive-technology users.
- Performance/load targets using representative matter/document volumes.
- Complete export expectations and client offboarding acceptance.

## Assurance gaps

Threat model, data-flow diagrams, independent penetration test, access-review/support-access procedures, restore exercises, incident tabletop, risk/subprocessor/BAA registers, control evidence, and SOC 2 readiness program remain required. None is implied by a passing software build.
