# California Workers’ Compensation Jurisdiction Pack

## Version contract

Every rule, proceeding type, form definition, official title, service requirement, and deadline calculation has a version, effective date, source authority, review date, and supersession link. Athena must not hard-code regulatory content without this envelope.

## Domain scope

- ADJ/WCAB identity, venue, judge, UAN, consolidation, and proceeding history.
- Status, priority, MSC/rating MSC, lien, expedited, trial, reconsideration, reopening, and settlement-approval proceedings.
- QME/AME panel, strike, appointment, report, supplemental request, MMI/P&S, WPI, apportionment, restrictions, future medical, and medical chronology.
- DWC forms, cover/separator sheets, proof of service, packet order/validation, transmission handoff, acceptance/rejection, correction, and resubmission identity.
- Records/subpoena, liens, settlement readiness, payment follow-up, closure, and reopening.

## Readiness contract

Proceeding and settlement readiness evaluate issues, discovery, medical records, service, exhibits, witnesses, adjuster availability, authority, interpreter, pretrial statement, client report, filing status, benefits, ratings, liens, forms, signatures, and attachments. Each result distinguishes missing data/document, inconsistency, client decision, court requirement, firm preference, and external dependency.

## Current evidence and limitation

The private pilot has California operations/docket views, source-linked QME facts, authority and filing approval gates, and an explicit EAMS-not-connected state. Versioned regulatory records, business-day rules, packet schemas, proof-of-service generation, third-party JET adapter, and return-status ingestion remain foundation work and require legal/content review before operational use.
